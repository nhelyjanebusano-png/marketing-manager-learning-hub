Hiring Post: Spelling, Thought & Process Check

The post itself is clear and internally consistent. The wording communicates a hands-on marketing operator/manager, not someone who only creates posts. The strongest signals are backlog ownership, multi-channel cadence, newsletter ownership, QA, project management and B2B experience.

One wording point to understand: "QA case studies and creative assets" means quality assurance—not writing case studies only. You should be able to inspect content before it goes live and catch factual, editorial, visual, link and brand issues.

"Own newsletter strategy, copywriting, and send cadence" signals end-to-end ownership: decide the purpose and editorial direction, write/edit, build the campaign, QA it, schedule/send it, then read the results.

"Build marketing from scratch" signals systems thinking: templates, SOPs, calendar, roles, approvals, metrics, tools and recurring routines—not simply more content.

Gap-to-Confidence Framework

  1. Understand: learn the concept and vocabulary.
  2. Observe: analyze how a real brand uses it.
  3. Build: create the asset/system yourself.
  4. Operate: run it for a week or simulated cycle.
  5. Measure: inspect KPI/output.
  6. Explain: present your reasoning as if reporting to a COO.

Backlog Recovery SOP

  1. Inventory every asset.
  2. Define "publish-ready".
  3. Tag by channel, campaign, pillar, format, owner and status.
  4. Score business impact, urgency and effort.
  5. Decide: publish, refresh, repurpose, park or archive.
  6. Build a realistic cadence.
  7. Assign owners and deadlines.
  8. Add QA/approval gates.
  9. Measure backlog age and on-time publishing weekly.

Case Study QA Method

When reading a case study, do not treat every sentence as equally proven.

  1. Context: company, audience, market and timeframe.
  2. Problem: measurable business/marketing problem.
  3. Baseline: what was happening before.
  4. Intervention: what changed.
  5. Evidence: metrics, methodology, sample and timeframe.
  6. Alternative explanations: seasonality, budget, audience changes, product changes, tracking changes.
  7. Transferability: what principle can be reused without copying blindly.
